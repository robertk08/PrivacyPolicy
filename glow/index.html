Privacy Policy for Glow

Effective November 22, 2026. This policy covers the Glow app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Glow is made by Berenike Krause ("we"). Contact: contact@feedcast.org.

Glow collects no data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting of its own, and it never talks to a server run by us or by anyone else on the internet. Glow only talks to your Glow controller on your local network.

1. What stays between your device and your controller

Your shows, lights, scenes and settings are stored on your Glow controller, not on our servers. Glow sends them to the controller and loads them from it over your local network. When you set up the controller, Glow sends it the name and password of the Wi-Fi network you choose, so the controller can join that network. If you set a controller password, Glow keeps a key derived from it in your device's keychain so you do not have to enter it again. Glow also remembers the address of the last controller it reached. None of this ever leaves your local network.

2. Permissions

  • Local network: Glow needs local network access to find your controller and talk to it over Wi-Fi.
  • Joining the controller's Wi-Fi: during setup, Glow asks to join the controller's own network, Glow Controller, so it can hand over your Wi-Fi details.

3. Files you share

When you export a show, Glow creates a file and hands it to the share sheet or the place you choose. When you import a show, Glow reads only the file you pick. Where that file goes is up to you.

4. Apple Home

If you add your controller to Apple Home, the pairing happens between the controller and your Home app. Apple handles that data under its own privacy policy. Glow is not involved.

5. Apple

Apple may collect information about app downloads and, if you agreed to share it with developers, crash reports and usage statistics. Apple shares these with us only in anonymous, aggregated form. See Apple's privacy policy.

6. Children

Glow collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy in Glow: contact@feedcast.org.
